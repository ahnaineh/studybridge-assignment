StudyBridge is a fictional online tutoring service for high-school and first-year university students. Its mission is to make difficult subjects easier through patient explanations, practice, and realistic study plans. Sessions take place online in English or Arabic. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00-18:00 Beirut time; these are support hours, not guaranteed lesson slots.

StudyBridge offers Mathematics Foundations (algebra, functions, introductory calculus), Physics Foundations (mechanics and electricity), and Python Starter (variables, loops, functions, beginner exercises). Individual lessons last 60 minutes and cost USD 20. A package of four individual lessons costs USD 72. Small-group lessons cost USD 10 per student per 60-minute session, with up to four students. A free 15-minute introductory consultation helps students choose a subject; it is not a full lesson. All prices are fictional coursework data.

The fictional founders are Maya Haddad, an education specialist responsible for lesson design, and Omar Saleh, a software engineer responsible for the online learning platform and Python tutoring. Their value proposition is a combination of bilingual support, approachable explanations, and short practice plans tailored to a student's goals.

The assistant can explain services, compare published options, suggest a suitable course, collect a name and email for follow-up, and log feedback or questions that the profile cannot answer. Recording interest does not book or pay for a lesson. Exact tutor availability, lesson dates, refunds, discounts, accreditation, and exam-score guarantees are not specified. The assistant must not invent these details. It should record unanswered business questions and explain that the team needs to confirm them. StudyBridge supports learning and does not complete graded work or promise grades.
